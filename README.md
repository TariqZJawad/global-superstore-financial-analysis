## 📖 The Story

### 🕵️‍♂️ Act I: The Illusion of Success (The Ask)
Global SuperStore was celebrating massive revenue milestones, yet executive leadership faced a glaring anomaly: **profits were inexplicably shrinking.** The objective was clear. I needed to dive into a massive dataset of global transactions to uncover the root causes of this financial bleeding.

### 🧹 Act II: Isolating the Variables (Data Preparation)
To find the truth, the data had to be spotless. I processed and isolated over 50,000 sales records, stripping away irrelevant variables and standardizing financial formats. This rigorous cleanup resulted in a high-integrity baseline, ensuring that every subsequent calculation was absolutely precise.

---

## 💡 The Investigation & Key Discoveries (Visible in the Dashboard)

By examining the dashboard above, three critical failure points in the business model become instantly visible:

### 🚩 1. Volume Does Not Equal Value (Bottom Right)
The **Tables** category is an absolute black hole for margins. Despite generating a massive **$757,000 in sales volume**, it is the sole product line causing a catastrophic net loss of **-$64,000**. The sheer volume was masking the logistical and pricing failures of this specific product.

### 🪤 2. The Discount Trap (Bottom Left)
Retail discount policies act as the silent killer of regional profit margins when left unchecked. The Southeast Asia market suffered collapsing profits due to an aggressive **27% average discount rate**. Conversely, Central Asia maintained excellent profitability through strict discipline, holding discounts at a healthy **7%**.

### 🌊 3. The Market Rhythm (Top)
Timing is everything in retail. By plotting sales over a 12-month trajectory, a severe seasonal slump emerges. The data shows a dramatic peak in Q4 (November/December), heavily contrasted by a drastic dip in mid-year (July). Capital was being tied up in stagnant inventory during the summer months.

---

## 🚀 The Resolution (Core Strategy)

Data is only as valuable as the decisions it drives. Based on the evidence, I proposed a three-pillar strategy to immediately stop the profit erosion:

1. **Pricing & Logistics (The Table Crisis):** Immediately renegotiate international shipping contracts for heavy items like Tables, or restructure their pricing model to absorb shipping costs.
2. **The 15% Cap (The Discount Fix):** Implement a strict discount cap of 10% - 15% in underperforming global regions to instantly recover lost margins.
3. **Smart Inventory (The Seasonal Shift):** Drastically reduce procurement orders before the July slump, redirecting capital to ramp up inventory scaling in late summer for the Q4 peak.
